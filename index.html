Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about chartering with Anand Travels.

Can we play our own music and connect via Bluetooth?
▼
Yes, absolutely! All our party coaches are equipped with high-end digital Bluetooth receivers, AUX cables, and USB connections. You have 100% control over the playlist, volume, and vibe throughout your journey.
Are tolls, fuel, and driver charges included in the price?
▼
Yes! Our quotes are fully transparent. Standard package rates include fuel for the agreed kilometers/hours, highway toll taxes, parking permits, and driver bata/allowance. No surprise charges at the end of the trip.
What is the policy for food, drinks, and snacks onboard?
▼
You are welcome to bring dry snacks, finger food, cake, and beverages onboard. We provide ice coolers and trash bins. We only request guests maintain reasonable cleanliness. Alcohol permits depend on local regional laws.
How far in advance should we reserve the bus?
▼
For peak wedding dates (November to February) and weekend party nights, we recommend booking 2 to 4 weeks in advance. For weekday events or corporate offsites, 5 to 7 days notice is usually sufficient.
Can we add custom decorations like banners or balloons?
▼
Yes! You can either arrive 30 minutes before departure to decorate the bus with your team, or select our Custom Decor Add-on during booking and our crew will have the bus pre-decorated for your celebration.
