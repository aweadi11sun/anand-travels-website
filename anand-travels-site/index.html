A legacy on the road since 1991

From one small beginning to journeys for thousands.

Three decades of hard work, family commitment and the trust of travellers have shaped the Anand Travels story.

Every long journey begins somewhere. For Anand Travels, it began in 1991 with a humble Bajaj Matador and a belief that every passenger deserved to travel with care.

Founded by Mr. Chandrakantji Dhoka, the business grew one trip, one family and one promise at a time. That foundation was carried forward by Mr. Ishwar Dhoka, whose efforts helped take the company into a new chapter. Today, Mr. Ronak Dhoka continues to lead Anand Travels with respect for the journey that came before him and ambition for the road ahead.

From that first small vehicle to a fleet of more than 145 buses, our story is about more than growth. It is about generations of dedication, the confidence of our customers, and the memories people make when they travel together.
